My apps & websites

Apps that help people read, learn and look up at the sky.

Qurany Piroz

قورئانی پیرۆز — پەڕتووکی خودا

iPhone · Android · Website · Free

The Holy Quran — The Book of God. A Kurdish app for reading and understanding the Holy Quran. It comes in 16 languages and brings together 47 tafsirs (explanations of the Quran) by scholars from around the world.

  • Read the whole Quran anywhere, even without an internet connection.
  • Test what you know with a Quran quiz, or play it live against friends.
  • Send any verse to a friend as a link that opens right in the app.

Pirsa Turn notes into a quiz

پرسا

iPhone · Android · Free

Turns your study material into a quiz. Choose a PDF or paste some text, pick how many questions you want, and start answering. A simple way to revise before an exam.

  • Six kinds of questions: multiple choice, true or false, written answers, putting things in order, filling in the blanks, and matching.
  • Works with Kurdish, Arabic and English, even photographed or scanned pages, and asks the questions in the same language as your notes.
  • In the Ardalani dialect of Kurdish, pirsa means “someone who seeks knowledge”.

03

CêIT Learn technology in Kurdish

جێ (cê) means “place”: a place to learn IT

Website · Free

A free website where Kurdish students learn about computers and technology, in simple English and Kurdish (Sorani). Every lesson can be read in English, in Kurdish, or both side by side.

  • Tap any technical word to see what it means in Kurdish and in English, with an example.
  • Learn by doing: build a computer network, program a robot, and see how a computer adds 2 + 2, right on the page.
  • Short lessons with quick questions to check yourself, and hand-picked videos.
  • Keeps working without internet once you've opened it. No sign-up and no ads.

04

Gospel of Barnabas

ئینجیلی بەرنابا · إنجيل برنابا

iPhone · iPad · Free

An app for reading the Gospel of Barnabas in English, Arabic and Kurdish. Switch language whenever you like and the whole app changes with it.

  • All 222 chapters, with Barnabas's introduction, an Islamic commentary on 44 chapters, and five articles about the book.
  • A new, complete Kurdish translation.
  • Make the text bigger or smaller, search, save your favourite chapters, and share them.
  • Works without internet. No sign-up and no ads.
Get it for iPhone & iPad
05

Zewyar Space station tracker

زەویار

iPhone

See where the International Space Station and China's Tiangong station are right now, live on a map. The name joins zewî (earth) and yar (friend): stations that circle the earth like a faithful friend.

  • Watch the stations move across the map, see how fast and how high they're flying, and where on Earth it's day or night.
  • Get a reminder before a station flies over your city, so you can step outside and see it with your own eyes.
  • Learn about Muslim astronauts, and how they find the qibla and prayer times in space.
  • Entirely in Kurdish.
Get it for iPhone
06

Zriwe Map of the night sky

زریوە — نەخشەی ئاسمان

iPhone · Free

Hold your phone up to the night sky and Zriwe shows you what you're looking at: the names of the stars, the shapes of the constellations, the planets, the Sun and the Moon.

  • Over 3,000 stars and nearly 100 constellations, plus galaxies and star clusters.
  • See the path the Sun and the Moon will take across the sky today.
  • Just point and look: the map turns as you move your phone.
