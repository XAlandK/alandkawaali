Aland Kawa Ali

کوردی الْعَرَبِيَّةُ

In the name of God, the Giver of Knowledge. My forebears, the age-old forebears of the Kurds, took pride in serving their land and their faith, and as far as I am able, I follow their path. Both are means; my Lord, the Lord of Mount Judi, is my goal. What matters is that, until my last breath, I can help the earth flourish by serving people, my land and my faith, with the support of the Giver of Knowledge.

Qurany Piroz قورئانی پیرۆز — پەڕتووکی خودا

Since 2017 — Free

The Holy Quran — The Book of God. A Kurdish app for reading and understanding the Holy Quran. It comes in 16 languages and brings together 47 tafsirs (explanations of the Quran) by scholars from around the world.

Website

MoreLess
  • Read the whole Quran anywhere, even without an internet connection.
  • Test what you know with a Quran quiz, or play it live against friends.
  • Send any verse to a friend as a link that opens right in the app.

Pirsa پرسا

Since 2025 — Free

Turns your study material into a quiz. Choose a PDF or paste some text, pick how many questions you want, and start answering. A simple way to revise before an exam.

Website

MoreLess
  • Six kinds of questions: multiple choice, true or false, written answers, putting things in order, filling in the blanks, and matching.
  • Works with Kurdish, Arabic and English, even photographed or scanned pages, and asks the questions in the same language as your notes.
  • In the Ardalani dialect of Kurdish, pirsa means “someone who seeks knowledge”.

CêIT

Since 2026 — Free

A free website where Kurdish students learn about computers and technology, in simple English and Kurdish (Sorani). Every lesson can be read in English, in Kurdish, or both side by side.

Website

MoreLess
  • جێ (cê) means “place”: a place to learn IT.
  • Tap any technical word to see what it means in Kurdish and in English, with an example.
  • Learn by doing: build a computer network, program a robot, and see how a computer adds 2 + 2, right on the page.
  • Short lessons with quick questions to check yourself, and hand-picked videos.
  • Keeps working without internet once you've opened it. No sign-up and no ads.

Zewyar زەویار

Since 2026

See where the International Space Station and China's Tiangong station are right now, live on a map. The name joins zewî (earth) and yar (friend): stations that circle the earth like a faithful friend.

iPhone

MoreLess
  • Watch the stations move across the map, see how fast and how high they're flying, and where on Earth it's day or night.
  • Get a reminder before a station flies over your city, so you can step outside and see it with your own eyes.
  • Learn about Muslim astronauts, and how they find the qibla and prayer times in space.
  • Entirely in Kurdish.

Rêga ڕێگا

Since 2026

A driving companion for BYD plug-in hybrid and electric cars in the Kurdistan Region, on the car's screen or a phone. It shows you the way, works out what the trip costs and, following BYD's own manuals, advises which driving mode to use.

Android (APK)

MoreLess
  • Turn-by-turn voice directions, spoken in Kurdish by a recorded voice. Works without internet once the Kurdistan map is downloaded.
  • Speed limits from OpenStreetMap, with a warning when you go over.
  • Suggests ECO, NORMAL or SPORT by what matters to you, with tips for steep climbs and long descents. Advice only: the app never controls the car.
  • Works out the trip cost on petrol and on electricity, and the cost of charging at home on the Runaki tariff.
  • In Kurdish, Arabic and English. Every figure says where it came from, and it never shows a made-up number.
  • Needs Android 7.0 or newer. It isn't on Google Play, so Android asks you to allow the install.
  • Rêga is Kurdish for “road”. An unofficial app, not affiliated with or endorsed by BYD.

Fuel Cost Calculator ژمێرەری تێچووی بەنزین

Since 2026 — Free

Estimates the Super petrol cost of a journey in a BYD Qin Plus DM-i. Pick a start and a destination on the map and it measures the road distance for you.

Open the calculator

MoreLess
  • Change the fuel consumption to see what faster or more demanding driving would cost.
  • Set the consumption in litres per 100 km or in km per litre, as an exact figure or as a range for a rough estimate.
  • Starts from a real measurement: 5.26 litres over 227 km, for 10,000 IQD.
  • Counts petrol only. Electricity isn't included.
